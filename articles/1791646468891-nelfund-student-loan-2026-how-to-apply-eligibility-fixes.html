NELFUND Student Loan 2026: How to Apply, Eligibility & Fixes

Believe · 10 October 2026

On this page

    Student loans in Nigeria are a huge relief for students and parents. Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND) has been set up to provide interest-free loans to eligible students in tertiary institutions. This article discusses the application process, eligibility criteria, and some of the common problems and how to resolve them.

    What Is NELFUND?


    The NELFUND scheme was established by the Student Loan (Access to Higher Education Act 2023) , signed in 2024, to provide students with interest-free loans for tuition and upkeep. The student loan is not a scholarship, but something that needs to be returned.

    Under the scheme, two types of support are on offer:

    Institutional charges : tuition and approved fees, payable directly to your institution.

    Upkeep allowance: a monthly stipend (reported as ₦20,000) , paid directly to your bank account.

    You may apply for either or both.

    Who Is Eligible for the NELFUND Student Loan?


    There are certain eligibility criteria which may change, so it is best to check on the official portal. In general, you will need to:

    Be a Nigerian citizen.

    Be admitted or enrolled in an approved tertiary institution on the NELFUND list.

    Your institution must have your student information verified on their NELFUND dashboard.

    Have a valid NIN, BVN and JAMB registration number.

    ⚠️ Warning
    • if your institution has not uploaded or verified your records on the NELFUND portal, your application will not be processed, even if you have filled out the details correctly.


    Documents and Information You Need

    Make sure you have the following documents and information ready when applying:

    • Valid email address and mobile number
    • National Identification Number (NIN)
    • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
    • JAMB registration number
    • Matriculation number (or admission/JAMB number as a fresh student)
    • Your admission letter, uploaded to the portal
    • A bank account in your name

    How to Apply for the NELFUND Student Loan (Step by Step)


    The process is entirely online. You can use the official portal ( https://nelf.gov.ng where you click on 'apply now'). It is advisable to type in the URL directly, rather than follow links from other websites. The steps are broadly as follows:

    • Create an account with a valid email address.
    • Verify your identity with your NIN, BVN and JAMB registration number.
    • Add your institution details (including your matriculation number or admission number).
    • Choose between a loan for institutional charges, upkeep allowance, or both.
    • Upload your admission letter and review.
    • Submit your application and monitor it on the portal.

    The online application process reportedly takes between 15 and 30 minutes, and is free of charge.

    📌 Deadlines: Application windows open and close. For the 2025/2026 session, the deadline was extended from January 31 to February 27, 2026. It is best to monitor the portal so that you do not miss the deadline window.

    How Repayment Works


    The loan scheme is interest-free, so you only repay the amount that you borrowed. Under the NELFUND Act, repayment is designed to begin after graduation and national service, and takes the form of a percentage of your income.

    It is important to read the repayment terms and conditions on the portal, in order to avoid any unpleasant surprises.


    Common NELFUND Problems and How to Fix Them


    1. "Student not found" or records missing

    The problem might be that your institution has not yet uploaded your information to the NELFUND portal. Contact your school's ICT unit or student affairs office to verify.

    2. NIN or BVN mismatch

    Your full name must match across your NIN, BVN, and your university records. If you have a discrepancy (name spelling or date of birth), address it with the relevant body (NIMC for NIN, your bank for BVN) before reapplying.

    3. Application stuck on "pending"

    This most often means that verification is still pending, or that your institution has not completed their part of the process. You can reapply after resolving the issue (do not create new applications in the meantime).

    4. Application rejected

    There are several reasons why an application can be rejected. These include incorrect information, an unverified record at your institution, or an ineligible institution or programme. If the cycle is still open, you can reapply after making the necessary corrections to your application. If not, students who are not approved can try applying again in a future cycle, if they are still eligible. It is important to contact your institution to ensure that your application has actually been submitted to the NELFUND portal.

    5. Upkeep not received

    Check that your bank details are correct and that the account is in your name.

    6. OTP or email not arriving

    Make sure to check your spam folder and that the email address is correct.

    ⚠️ Warning
    • ⚠️ Never pay anyone to 'approve' your loan. The process is free of charge, and any agent asking for money is a scam.

    • ⚠️ Never share your BVN, OTP, or password with anyone claiming to work for NELFUND.⚠️ Never share your BVN, OTP, or password with anyone claiming to work for NELFUND.
    • ⚠️ Beware of rumours. Claims that the upkeep allowance has been increased to ₦150,000 have been circulated online, but have since been debunked by the NELFUND and a beneficiaries' association. It is best to seek out the official portal or verified social media accounts, before believing a viral post.


    Pro Tips for a Smooth Application


    Apply early. The portal can get very busy as the deadline approaches.

    Confirm with your institution first that your records have been uploaded.

    Use your own email address and phone number, rather than a café or friend's details.

    Make sure to match your full name across your NIN, BVN and admission documents.

    Always keep a digital copy of your admission letter, matric number and confirmation email.

    Only borrow what you need. Every naira has to be repaid.

    Follow the official NELFUND pages for any announcements about the application window or changes to the scheme.

    ❓ FAQ
    Question:
    Is the NELFUND loan really interest-free?
    Answer:
    Yes. You repay only the amount that you borrowed.
    Question:
    Is NELFUND only for fresh students?
    Answer:
    Fresh students can apply using their JAMB or admission number, as long as their institution is on the NELFUND list.
    Question:
    Can I apply for upkeep only?
    Answer:
    Yes. You can apply for either institutional fees, upkeep allowance, or both.
    Question:
    Does NELFUND cover postgraduate programmes?
    Answer:
    The scheme has focused on undergraduate and tertiary students. It is best to check the portal for any updates.

    Question:
    Do I need a guarantor?
    Answer:
    NO, Requirements may change, so it is best to check the portal for the latest information.
    Question:
    Can I reapply if I am rejected?
    Answer:
    Yes, in a future cycle, if you are still eligible and the application window is still open.
    Question:
    Is it free to apply?
    Answer:
    Yes. Anyone asking for payment is not authorised to act on behalf of NELFUND.

    Final Thoughts

    NELFUND can make the difference between continuing your studies and dropping out. The key is to prepare ahead of time: verify your institution and records, match your NIN and BVN details, and make sure to apply well before the deadline. Avoid scams, and read the repayment terms and conditions carefully. Apply only via the official portal.
