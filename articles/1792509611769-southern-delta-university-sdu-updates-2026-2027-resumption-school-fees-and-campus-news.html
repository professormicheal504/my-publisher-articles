Southern Delta University (SDU) Updates: 2026/2027 Resumption, School Fees, and Campus News

    Are you an aspirant, newly admitted student, or returning scholar of Southern Delta University (SDU), Ozoro? If yes, staying informed on official academic announcements is critical to planning your semester.

    Formerly operating as the Delta State University of Science and Technology (DSUST), Southern Delta University has undergone massive transformations under Vice-Chancellor Professor Sunny Ijirhevwe Awhefeada. From cutting-edge structural expansions to essential academic timelines, here is everything you need to know about the 2026/2027 academic session.


      Official SDU Resumption Dates for 2026/2027 Session

          The Senate of Southern Delta University has officially approved and released the academic calendar. Activities have commenced in phases to ensure seamless screening and registration across both the Ozoro (Main) and Orerokpe campuses.
    • Academic Staff Resumption: Monday, October 5, 2026
    • Fresh Students (Newly Admitted): Monday, October 12, 2026 (Screening and registration are currently underway)
    • End of Registration for New Students: Friday, October 23, 2026
    • Returning Students Resumption: Sunday, October 25, 2026

       SDU School Fees Update: Management Debunks Fake News

    There have been viral social media reports claiming a steep hike in tuition and auxiliary fees for the 2026/2027 session.
    The university management, through the Registrar, Mrs. Ejiroghene Etobro, has completely debunked these rumors. SDU explicitly stated that no fee increase has been authorized and warned that malicious actors went as far as forging the university's official stamp. Students should ignore unauthorized fee schedules online and strictly verify tuition details via the official SDU E-Portal.

       Massive Campus Infrastructure Upgrades

    The Delta State Government, led by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori, is heavily investing in turning SDU into a premium technological hub. Key infrastructure upgrades completed for the 2026 academic year include:
    1. Faculty Complex Additions: The newly commissioned Faculty of Computing Complex and the state-of-the-art Faculty of Medical Sciences Building.
    2. Advanced Engineering Workshops: The Pa James Odhokpa Izomor Engineering Workshops—a suite of 10 fully equipped technical workshops donated by Engr. Godwin Izomor.
    3. Free Campus Wi-Fi: Thanks to a recent digital intervention initiative, SDU students now enjoy free campus-wide internet access to aid research and digital learning.
    4. Hostel Expansions: Construction of massive new hostels to alleviate student accommodation pressures.
